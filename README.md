# gameception

You manage an empty galaxy that slowly fills with alien populations, each with unique environmental needs. To keep them alive, you design and maintain planets under constant time pressure — but success creates new problems. As populations grow, their demands escalate and planets begin to interact, leading to jealousy and conflict. The challenge is balancing creativity, strategy, and prioritisation across multiple worlds before everything collapses!

## Gameplay
The game begins in an empty galaxy.

Alien populations arrive one by one in UFOs, each with unique environmental needs. For each population, the player must create a new planet and configure its ecosystem to support that species.

Every ecosystem setting (such as atmosphere, resources, or climate) requires ongoing attention and has time-based constraints. If the player does not respond in time, the population begins to decline.

As alien populations grow, their demands increase, requiring more frequent and complex management. At the same time, planets begin to interact — alien populations may become jealous of more successful planets, leading to interplanetary conflict.

The player must strategically balance growth, maintenance, and conflict across multiple planets simultaneously.

The game ends when the player can no longer keep up with the demands and all alien populations die, or when the global time limit (e.g. 10 minutes) is reached.
